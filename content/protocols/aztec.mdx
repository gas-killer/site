Gas Killer could cut the gas cost of an Aztec epoch proof submission by about **83%**: roughly 1,390,000–1,455,000 of the 1,675,000–1,752,000 gas each `submitEpochRootProof` spends today. We measured this on real Ethereum mainnet submissions to the Aztec rollup in October 2026. The rollup receives about 258 of these a day, at a median of 1.75M gas, which is more proof-verification gas than any other contract we have measured.

## Where the saving comes from

Each submission verifies an epoch proof. In the call trace of [0x99b5d08e…](https://etherscan.io/tx/0x99b5d08e92288d59672f29366b5bdb500d47e8c96d18bdfd6b8eed2dbcf1f131), 721,697 gas is a read-only call to Aztec's Honk verifier, 152,456 gas is precompiles, including the point-evaluation precompile, and 12,761 gas is another read. The rest is the rollup's own bookkeeping.

- **What gets cheaper:** the proof verification runs off-chain. On-chain, only the rollup's resulting storage changes and events are applied.
- **What integration involves:** the Aztec rollup contract would use the Gas Killer SDK.

## How we measured

We replayed each transaction as Gas Killer would settle it and compared the gas against what it actually cost.

- **Real data:** real mainnet `submitEpochRootProof` calls from October 2026, replayed at the block they ran in.
- **Includes all costs:** the gas saved is net of the signature check and every storage write.
- **An estimate:** Aztec has not integrated Gas Killer, so the numbers estimate a future integration.
- **Large traces:** each transaction's trace runs to several gigabytes, so we measured these with a build of the analyzer that streams the trace. The browser analyzer can't run them yet.
