Gas Killer could cut the gas cost of a Morpho VaultV2 deposit, withdrawal or redemption by about **14–40%**, up to about 113,000 gas on each one. Before it can price a share, the vault reads its allocation and accrued interest across markets, and that read is what Gas Killer removes. We measured this on real Ethereum mainnet calls to vaults from three curators: Sentora RLUSD, Steakhouse USDC and Gauntlet USDC.
