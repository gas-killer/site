Gas Killer could cut the gas cost of ynETH MAX's `processAccounting` by about **76–79%**: roughly 565,000 of the 715,000–745,000 gas each update spends today. We measured this on real Ethereum mainnet transactions to the ynETH MAX vault. The saving comes from pricing the vault's strategy positions, and it needs no change to what the accounting update writes.

## What we measured

All five `processAccounting` transactions we measured save over 75%, and the saving is almost the same every time.
