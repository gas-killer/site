## Where the saving comes from

The saving comes from the proof check. A Groth16 verification is one pairing (181,000 gas) plus 4 elliptic-curve multiplications and 4 additions, about 205,600 gas, and it runs in the PoolVault inside a read-only call.

- **What gets cheaper:** the proof verification runs off-chain. On-chain, only the new commitments, the spent-note markers, the transfers and the events are applied.
- **What stays the same:** the proof's statement, the association-set rules and every check the vault enforces.
- **What integration involves:** the PoolVault would use the Gas Killer SDK. Deposits that arrive through the router or the entrypoint reach it through regular calls, so those contracts would integrate too, as a nested settlement.

## How we measured

We replayed each transaction as Gas Killer would settle it and compared the gas against what it actually cost.

- **Real data:** real mainnet calls to the PoolVault, replayed at the block they ran in.
- **Includes all costs:** the gas saved is net of the signature check and every storage write.
- **An estimate:** Privacy Pools has not integrated Gas Killer, so the numbers estimate a future integration.
